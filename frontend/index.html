MODEL REGISTRY

Detection & segmentation

Verified ONNX weights with CPU inference and real instance masks.

Training and deployment

The bundled model detects generic vehicle classes. Aerial camera angles, small objects and poor visibility can reduce accuracy. Validate on your own held-out videos before deployment.

Use the included scripts/train.py, scripts/evaluate.py and scripts/register_model.py to train, evaluate and register trusted YOLOv8 ONNX models. Restart the service after changing the registry.

Model code and weights use AGPL-3.0. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for the source and licensing information.
